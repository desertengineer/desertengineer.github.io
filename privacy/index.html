Data Collection and Use

Bekeirat Software may collect, store, and use your personal information in connection with developing, improving, and providing our products, software applications, whether on third-party sites or as mobile applications), websites, and other services (collectively, "Services").

We operate on the fundamental principle that your personal data belongs to you. We are committed to transparency regarding what data we collect, ensuring it is only processed for legitimate business purposes, and providing a secure environment for your information.

Policy Scope and Consent

This privacy policy ("Privacy Policy") explains how personal and technical information is collected, used, and shared by us in compliance with applicable global privacy laws, including the GDPR and CCPA. Please read it carefully, as all of our Services are governed by this Privacy Policy.

By using or accessing any Service, you agree to the collection, processing, and retention of your data as described here. You may withdraw your consent or manage your preferences at any time by contacting us through the methods outlined below.

Types of Information We Collect

To provide and optimize our Services, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide directly: Personal identification information such as your name, email address, or communication preferences when you contact support or subscribe to updates.
  • Automatically collected data: Usage data, gameplay progress, and in-app analytics to help us improve game mechanics and user experience.
  • Device and technical information: IP addresses, device types, operating system versions, and unique device identifiers (such as mobile advertising IDs).

How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following lawful purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and provide the core functionality of our games and applications.
  • To personalize your experience and deliver tailored content or targeted advertisements.
  • To respond to your customer service requests and communicate important updates.
  • To monitor app performance, detect bugs, prevent fraud, and ensure platform security.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

We partner with third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads) to serve advertisements in games, allowing us to keep the service free. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, and mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Apple IDFA or Android Advertising ID) to serve relevant, contextual, or personalized ads.

You can control or reset your ad tracking preferences at any time directly through your device's operating system settings (e.g., disabling "Allow Apps to Request to Track" on iOS, or opting out of "Ads Personalization" on Android/Windows).

In-App Purchases and Payments

Our applications may support digital in-app purchases. All monetary transactions, credit card handling, and payment processing are securely managed directly by the respective platform's Store Commerce System (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Microsoft Store). Bekeirat Software does not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers or billing profiles.

Children’s Data Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Our Services are designed for a general audience. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global regulations, we do not knowingly collect, request, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information to us without verifiable consent, please contact us immediately at bekerat.mawlay@gmail.com so we can take steps to securely delete the data from our records.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share your data in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • With trusted third-party service providers (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics, and ad networks) who assist in our operations and are bound by strict data processing agreements.
  • To comply with valid legal processes, governmental requests, or applicable laws.
  • To protect the rights, property, and physical safety of Bekeirat Software, our users, or the public.

Data Retention and International Transfers

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Because we operate globally, your information may be transferred to, and processed in, countries other than the country in which you are resident. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for cross-border data transfers in compliance with applicable laws.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal information (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Opt-Out: Withdraw consent for specific data processing, including opting out of targeted advertising or the "sale/sharing" of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please be aware that no electronic storage system or method of data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as 100% secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last update" date at the top of this page or by providing a prominent notice within our applications.
